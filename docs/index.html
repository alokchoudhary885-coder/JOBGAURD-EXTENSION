How JobGuard Calculates Risk

Unlike opaque AI wrappers, JobGuard uses a transparent, explainable 12-factor mathematical engine that runs 100% locally in your browser.

Critical Scam Flags

  • +35 Registration, training, uniform, laptop, or processing fee extortion.
  • +30 Premature bank account, UPI PIN, or debit card verification requests.

Warning Indicators

  • +15 Recruiter uses free webmail (@gmail, @yahoo) instead of company domain.
  • +15 Directing candidates to personal WhatsApp or Telegram channels.
  • +15 Unrealistic salary (>₹50k/mo) for no-experience or data-entry roles.
  • +10 Instant offer letter promised without structured interviews.

Positive Trust Credits

  • −10 Authentic corporate email domain verified against employer.
  • −10 Hosted on verified Enterprise ATS (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday) or Schema.org.
